## Structural Problems

**1. No equity position stop-loss module exists.**

*Mechanism:* The system has no `equity_stop_loss_pct` or `max_unrealized_loss_pct` parameter at the individual position level. When an equity holding moves against the position, no automated response is triggered regardless of magnitude. FJET entered at ~$5.70, fell to ~$1.71–$1.84 (approximately −68%), and the system generated zero exit signals across the entire quarter — July through September.

*Evidence:* FJET flagged as the dominant portfolio drag in every weekly summary from W28 through W39 — a minimum of 12 consecutive weeks. Unrealized loss grew from approximately −$5,769 (W28 open) to −$17,697 (quarter-end). The July monthly review explicitly named the missing parameter. The September monthly review confirmed the same gap was still absent. No commit in the quarter's 8-entry changelog added this module.

*What must change:* Implement `equity_stop_loss_pct` as a position-level parameter in the risk engine's position ledger. Wire it into the execution loop's pre-trade evaluation so that a breach (e.g., −20% from entry) generates a hard sell order, not a log entry. This is a new module, not a config adjustment.

---

**2. The `book_loss` circuit breaker cannot distinguish position scale.**

*Mechanism:* `max_book_unrealized_loss_pct = 15%` is a binary gate. When it fires, it blocks every outbound trade regardless of the new trade's notional size, defined-risk nature, or relationship to the position causing the breach. It cannot allow a $500-collateral 5-delta CSP while blocking a $10,000 naked position. Once FJET pushed unrealized losses past 15% of book, the system was permanently frozen with no mechanism to generate income while holding 88.7% in cash.

*Evidence:* The circuit breaker fired 8/8 sessions in W38 and every session of W39. APA (IVR 80%), AA (IVR 68–74%), and GEO (IVR 56–59%) were blocked despite being small, defined-risk, unrelated to FJET. The September summary explicitly names this as a structural deficiency rather than appropriate conservatism. The breaker preserved capital from new losses but simultaneously made recovery impossible, because the only income-generation mechanism available (CSP premium) was shut off.

*What must change:* Replace the binary gate in the pre-trade gate evaluation logic with a tiered structure. At 15–20% unrealized book loss: block new positions with collateral above a threshold (e.g., $2,000). At 20–25%: block all new positions except BTC/close orders. Below 15%: normal operation. The config parameter is `max_book_unrealized_loss_pct`; a second parameter `tiered_gate_max_new_notional` must be added.

**3. The learning loop does not detect behavioral or code-logic gaps.**

*Mechanism:* The v2.1 learning loop processes trade-outcome feedback. It has no mechanism to surface findings of the form "this module fires but produces no downstream action" or "this position has no stop-loss and has been underwater for N weeks." Structural bugs that prevent trades from being placed — the policy execution handoff, the loop/deduplication bug, the missing equity stop — are invisible to a P&L-feedback loop because they produce no trade records to learn from.

*Evidence:* The loop/deduplication bug (W29–W31), the policy signal handoff gap (W29–W31), and the FJET stop absence (W28–W39) were all present before v2.1 deployed on July 6 and all present at quarter-end on September 30. The learning loop had 12+ weeks to surface these findings and produced no behavioral change on any of them. The July monthly review explicitly states: "the learning loop produced no measurable behavioral change across four weeks."

*What must change:* Add a separate structural-audit layer to the learning loop that runs weekly and checks: (a) signals logged with no downstream order within N sessions, (b) positions open beyond a defined age with no stop configured, (c) modules logging skip-decisions more than a threshold number of times per session. This layer must write findings to the same queue as trade-outcome feedback, not a separate log file. Config: add `learning_loop.structural_audit_enabled = true` and `learning_loop.signal_conversion_threshold_sessions = 3`.

**4. The wheel engine's candidate universe is too narrow and not dynamically supplemented.**

*Mechanism:* The wheel engine operates from a fixed candidate pool. When that pool is blocked by earnings clusters, IV floors, or sector restrictions, the system has no fallback. The research pipeline (NotebookLM layer) identifies high-IVR alternatives but has no programmatic handoff to the wheel engine. The two pipelines are parallel, not connected.

*Evidence:* W31: AVAV IVR 100%, RKLB IVR 100%, PLTR IVR 96%, CAT IVR 84% — zero trades because the primary pool was earnings-locked and no secondary list existed. W30: CEG IVR 68%, not in candidate pool, no blocking condition logged. Research layer identified SKHY (W29), CEG/CCJ (W30), AVAV/CAT/VST (W31) — none consumed by the wheel engine. This pattern recurred across all four July weeks and into August.

*What must change:* Implement `secondary_csp_watchlist` as a configurable parameter in `wheel/config`. Populate it with a minimum of 10 tickers that are non-earnings-correlated to the primary pool and pre-approved for CSP writing. Add a handoff function from the research pipeline to this watchlist so that IVR-flagged tickers from the research layer are evaluated by the wheel engine within one session. The universe screen commit (2026-08-21) may be the correct location for this logic.

**5. Strike selection has no price-awareness or loss-compounding guard.**

*Mechanism:* The CSP strike selection process can write near-the-money puts on high-volatility single names and re-enter the same name after an initial loss. The CC engine submits strikes without checking whether they are above the current market price. Both failures compound realized losses on names that are already underwater.

*Evidence:* CCJ received two separate CSP entries (P00090, P00098), both went deep ITM, total loss −$410. MP accumulated −$569 across 7 trades (3/7 wins). FJET CC submissions at $4.50, $6.00, $10.00 against a $1.81 stock — none filled, flagged W39. KTOS: 0/5 wins, −$620, with a BTC execution failure layered on top. Four of the six worst tickers this quarter share the same pattern: high-volatility name, multiple entries, no per-ticker position limit preventing re-entry after a loss.

*What must change:* (a) Add `strike_selection_delta_max` for high-volatility names (define "high-volatility" as 30-day IV > 60%) — tighten to delta 0.20 or lower. (b) Add `per_ticker_max_open_loss_usd` parameter: if a ticker's combined realized + unrealized loss exceeds this threshold, block new entries on that ticker. (c) In the CC engine, add a price-awareness guard: no strike may be submitted above `current_price * cc_strike_max_multiplier` (e.g., 1.10 — strikes must be within 10% above current price).

## What Needs To Be Worked On

**1. Implement the equity position stop-loss module.** *(Module: `risk/position_ledger.py` or equivalent; parameter: `equity_stop_loss_pct`)*

This is the single highest-impact change available. FJET at −68% with no stop is responsible for −$17,697 in unrealized losses — nearly the entire quarter's equity decline. A −20% hard stop from entry would have exited the position in W28 at an estimated loss of approximately −$3,100 instead of −$17,697, preserving roughly $14,000 in capital. Wire the stop check into the execution loop so it generates a market sell order, not a log entry. Measure: unrealized loss on any single position must not exceed `equity_stop_loss_pct × entry_cost` after implementation. Set initial value at 20%.

**2. Tier the `book_loss` circuit breaker.** *(Module: pre-trade gate evaluation; parameters: `max_book_unrealized_loss_pct`, add `tiered_gate_max_new_notional`)*

The current binary gate made it impossible to generate any income for at least two full weeks in September while 88.7% of capital sat idle. APA, AA, and GEO were all blocked despite representing defined-risk, small-notional trades. A tiered breaker (block positions > $2,000 collateral when book loss is 15–20%; block all new positions except closes above 20%) would have allowed income generation while still preventing capital from being recklessly deployed. Measure: count of IV scanner signals converted to orders per week; target non-zero when book loss is 15–20% and valid sub-threshold setups exist. This is priority 2 because it is blocked by priority 1: fix the stop-loss first to shrink the unrealized loss, then retune the breaker threshold.

**3. Resolve KTOS BTC execution failure and audit the order-confirmation path.** *(Module: `execution/order_manager.py` or equivalent BTC retry logic)*

`KTOS261024P00052000` triggered BTC 4+ times with no confirmed fill at −852% loss (~$1,790/contract). Position status was unknown entering the following week. This is an open, unquantified liability. Audit the order confirmation and state reconciliation logic: confirm whether BTC filled; if not, determine why reprice attempts did not complete. Add a maximum reprice attempt count with an escalation path (alert/manual flag) when the limit is reached. Measure: every BTC order must reach a terminal state (filled or explicitly flagged for manual review) within one session.

**4. Fix the policy signal execution handoff.** *(Module: policy monitor downstream of `status: SIGNAL` log; the order-placement pathway is entirely absent)*

90+ signals fired over at least three weeks and zero converted to orders. This is not a signal quality problem — defense supply chain EOs and DPA/critical minerals signals in W30–W31 were named as legitimate multi-day themes. The pathway from `status: SIGNAL` to an order object does not exist. Implement it. The fix should be testable by replaying a logged signal through the policy monitor and confirming an order object is generated. Measure: signal-to-order conversion rate, reported weekly; target > 0% on signals that pass the pre-trade gate.

**5. Add `per_ticker_max_open_loss_usd` and tighten `strike_selection_delta_max` for high-IV names.** *(Module: `wheel/strike_selector.py`; parameters: `per_ticker_max_open_loss_usd`, `strike_selection_delta_max_high_vol`)*

KTOS (0/5, −$620), RKLB (1/3, −$1,891), MP (3/7, −$569), CCJ (0/2, −$410) all share the same compounding pattern: re-entry on an already-losing name, near-the-money strikes on a high-volatility ticker. A per-ticker loss cap blocks re-entry once a ticker has exceeded a threshold (suggested: −$300 combined realized + unrealized). Tightening delta for high-IV names (30-day IV > 60%) to 0.20 or lower reduces the probability of deep-ITM assignment on initial entry. Measure: no single ticker should accumulate more than `per_ticker_max_open_loss_usd` in a rolling 90-day window.

**6. Wire the research pipeline to `secondary_csp_watchlist` and implement the watchlist parameter.** *(Module: `wheel/universe.py`; parameter: `secondary_csp_watchlist`)*

When the primary candidate pool is earnings-locked, the system has no fallback. The research layer identified high-IVR alternatives in three of four July weeks; none were consumed by the wheel engine. A minimum 10-ticker secondary watchlist, automatically populated from research-layer IVR flags, ensures the system can write premium when the primary pool is blocked. Measure: count of sessions with zero wheel candidates when at least one ticker on the secondary list has IVR > 50%; target: zero such sessions after implementation.

**7. Fix the wheel engine loop/deduplication bug.** *(Module: `wheel/engine.py` skip-decision logging; add per-ticker-per-session deduplication)*

Skip-decision logs fire 150–320 times per session instead of once per ticker. This inflates daily insight counts to 1,400–1,755, makes genuine decision logs unsearchable, and was confirmed as a bug in W30 and W31. The fix is a session-scoped deduplication guard on skip-decision logging. This is listed seventh because it does not directly affect the equity curve — it affects observability and the reliability of performance monitoring. Measure: skip-decision log count per session should not exceed the number of tickers evaluated.
