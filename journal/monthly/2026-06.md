## Lessons Carried Forward

- **A stop-loss configured at -250% that triggers at -852%, -900%, and -9,100% is not a stop-loss; it is a label on a non-functional module.** Evidence: three confirmed breaches across three weeks (ABT W24, KTOS W26, AADX W27). Until the monitor loop is confirmed to run intraday on every position, no short put should be opened on any name with beta > 1.0.

- **The wheel engine opening and position_manager closing the same ticker in the same session is not a timing quirk — it is proof that the two strategies do not share position state.** Evidence: W24 (ABT), W26 (ALB, Monday 6/22 multi-name), W27 (6/29 AM+PM double-fire). Each same-day open/close costs premium + commissions with zero income retained. The expected cost per occurrence is approximately $100–$500 in realized losses plus bid-ask drag.

- **The policy monitor's signal quality is sufficient for trade generation; the conversion failure is architectural, not informational.** Evidence: PLTR, LHX, RTX, LDOS received dual L1 confirmation in W24 (within 17 minutes) and no capital was deployed. The same week, the wheel sold CSPs on ABT (no policy signal, -900% stop) and XOM (-250% stop). The system's highest-conviction signals and its actual capital deployment are pointing in opposite directions every week.

- **Adding a recent IPO to the wheel watchlist without a minimum price-history and options-liquidity gate is a way to manufacture catastrophic losses.** Evidence: AADX — $0.05 premium collected, $4.60 mark intraday, -$455 realized at -9,100%. The 2026-06-12 commit added AADX to the matrix; the 2026-06-16 commit added it to the `no_auto_manage` exclusion. Neither commit prevented the loss because the loss happened at entry, not in management.

- **Research coverage and wheel coverage must overlap, or the research layer has no risk-management value.** Evidence: five consecutive weeks in which NotebookLM produced signals exclusively on non-optionable IPO names while the wheel traded CCJ, VST, CEG, KTOS, PLTR, AADX with zero research input. A research pipeline that never covers an active position cannot flag that position as dangerous.
