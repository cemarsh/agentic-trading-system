## Lessons Carried Forward

- **A 100% win rate on closed trades ($460 realized) is meaningless when unrealized losses are 38× larger ($17,696).** The reporting framing of "3 wins, 0 losses" actively obscures the state of the book. Win rate on closed trades cannot be used as a health signal when the largest position has no stop and cannot close.

- **A circuit breaker that cannot distinguish a $500 defined-risk trade from a $10,000 naked position is misconfigured, not conservative.** Evidence: APA at IVR 80% and AA at IVR 68–74% were blocked alongside genuinely dangerous new positions. The breaker preserved capital in the sense that it prevented new losses; it also prevented the only mechanism the system has to generate income while waiting for FJET to resolve.

- **The system has no equity stop-loss at all.** This is not a tuning problem — it is a missing module. FJET moved from entry (~$5.70) to ~$1.71–$1.84 with no automated response. Every week this persists is additional evidence that the gap is structural, not situational.

- **Infrastructure commits during a drawdown are noise unless they address the drawdown's cause.** The two September commits (mypy cleanup, whale source backoff) were both maintenance items. Neither touched `max_book_unrealized_loss_pct`, the equity stop module, the CC strike filter, or the KTOS BTC execution path. The reader's feeling that nothing is improving is, for September, factually correct.

- **A scanner that produces signals with no downstream execution path is a logging tool, not a trading tool.** The IV scanner correctly identified APA, AA, and GEO as high-conviction candidates across multiple sessions. Zero of these signals produced a trade. Until the `book_loss` gate is tiered or the FJET position is resolved, the scanner's output is inert.
