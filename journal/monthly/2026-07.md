## Lessons Carried Forward

- **A risk engine that only blocks entries cannot generate positive expectancy.** With a profit factor of 0.16 for the month, the v2.1 engine correctly suppressed bad new trades but left existing bad positions intact and generated zero new premium revenue. Blocking is necessary but insufficient — the system needs an execution pathway, not just a gate. *Evidence: 9 closed trades, 7 losses, $0 new CSP revenue in weeks W29–W31.*

- **Strike selection on high-beta names (CCJ, MP) at or near spot is the root cause of the realized loss month.** CCJ lost $410 across two positions; both went deep ITM requiring BTC at loss. MP lost $324 at -119.6% on the short put. The system has no `min_otm_distance` enforcement specifically for volatile names. *Evidence: CCJ and MP together account for $734 of $1,357 gross losses — 54% of all gross losses.*

- **A position without a defined stop or hedge rule will drift to maximum pain.** FJET had no stop-loss config parameter, no covered-call trigger, and no partial-exit logic. The result was a -40.7% drawdown on a ~$26K position with zero defensive response over four weeks. The v2.1 engine never addressed existing positions — only new entries. *Evidence: FJET unrealized P&L trajectory: -$5,769 → -$8,579 → -$10,612 → -$9,745 across W28–W31.*

- **When the entire primary wheel candidate pool shares an earnings calendar cluster, the system goes to zero revenue.** W31 demonstrated this directly: every ticker in the eligible universe was earnings-blocked for Aug 14 expiry, producing 150+ skip loops and zero trades. The secondary pool (CEG, AVAV, CAT, VST) existed in the IV scanner output but was not wired to the wheel engine. *Evidence: Zero CSPs written W31 despite IVR readings of AVAV 100%, CAT 84%, VST 51–73%.*

- **Log inflation from deduplication failures makes the system appear more active than it is and obscures real signal.** 1,400–1,755 "insight" events/day in W31 were predominantly loop artifacts. This creates false confidence in system activity and makes root-cause analysis harder. *Evidence: W30 MP earnings-skip logged 170x in one session; W31 wheel engine generating 150+ identical skips/session.*
