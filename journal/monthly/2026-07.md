## Lessons Carried Forward

- **A profit factor of 0.16 on 9 closed trades is not bad luck — it is a sizing and strike-selection problem.** Avg loss ($193.86) is 1.78× avg win ($109.00). CCJ received two separate CSP entries (P00090 and P00098) both of which went deep ITM. Writing near-the-money puts on a high-volatility uranium name is the documented root cause. The lesson: `strike_selection_delta` for volatile single names must be tightened, and per-ticker position limits must prevent doubling down on an already-underwater name.

- **An unrealized loss of -$9,745 on a single equity position with no stop defined is a system design failure, not a market outcome.** FJET moved against the position for the entire month. The market did its job. The system had no `equity_stop_loss_pct` parameter, no covered-call mitigation rule, and a broken share-availability accounting model that locked 93% of shares. The lesson is falsifiable: if a `max_unrealized_loss_pct` parameter at the position level had been set to, say, -15%, the position would have been cut before the end of W28, saving approximately $4,000–$6,000 in additional drawdown.

- **Regime NEUTRAL held all four weeks and the system has no productive behavior defined for NEUTRAL + low IVR.** When IV rank is 0–16% across screened names (W28 confirmed across ABT, SHLD, LDOS, CVX), no CSP should be written — the system correctly enforced this. But NEUTRAL + low IVR with no fallback behavior means the system simply does nothing for multi-week stretches. The lesson: define explicit idle-state rules (e.g., evaluate longer-dated CSPs, shift to equity entries, or explicitly park cash) rather than defaulting to paralysis.

- **The learning loop in v2.1 did not learn.** The same bugs identified in W28 (loop deduplication, policy execution handoff, FJET stop absence) were present unchanged in W31. Either the learning loop has no mechanism to surface structural code/config gaps — only trade-outcome feedback — or it was not running effectively. The lesson: learning loops that only process trade P&L cannot identify bugs that prevent trades from being placed in the first place.

- **High IVR is not enough to generate revenue if the wheel engine's candidate pool is empty.** W31 had AVAV at IVR 100%, RKLB at 100%, PLTR at 96%, CAT at 84% — and zero trades. The blocking condition was a common earnings-calendar cluster around Aug 14. The lesson is operational: the wheel engine needs a secondary watchlist of non-earnings-correlated tickers, configurable as `secondary_csp_watchlist`, so that when the primary pool is locked out, the system has pre-approved alternatives to evaluate.
